# ASTRA STYLIX: first content pilot

Accounts: TikTok @astrastylix; Instagram @astra_stylix; X @aelabirpistache (display name: @Aela_Stylix).
Status: scripts and captions prepared; videos not rendered or published.
Audience hypothesis: English-speaking adults interested in personal jewelry and styling.
Goal: learn which designs motivate saves, qualified comments and profile visits.

## Production rules

Use owned or licensed assets only. Confirm provenance before export. Existing repository images are candidates, not proof of publication rights.
For unproduced designs, keep `DESIGN CONCEPT - NOT YET AVAILABLE` legible throughout. Do not describe renders as samples or customer footage. Use platform AI labels when required. Do not claim material, durability, stock, scarcity or dispatch dates without evidence.
Export vertical 1080x1920, H.264; keep essential text away from platform controls. Make a clean master without a platform watermark. Add separately licensed commercial audio for each platform or use original narration.
Do not depict the known inaccurate GLB as a functioning product try-on. A concept preview is not a fit or size guarantee.

## Video 1: choose the first design (15 seconds)

Assets: two distinct owned design images, clearly labeled A and B. Use neutral descriptive names after visual verification.
0-2 seconds: both designs visible. Text: `Which would you actually wear: A or B?`
2-6 seconds: slow crop into A, then return to full piece. Text: `A`
6-10 seconds: same treatment for B. Text: `B`
10-15 seconds: both full pieces. Text: `Help us choose what to sample first.`
Voiceover: `We are choosing the first designs to sample for ASTRA STYLIX. Which would you actually wear, A or B? Tell us what made you choose.`
Caption: `Two concepts. Your everyday style. Which should we sample first, A or B? These are design previews, not finished products or items currently for sale. #JewelryDesign #PersonalStyle #AstraStylix`
Cover: `WHICH WOULD YOU WEAR?`

## Video 2: a personal milestone (16 seconds)

## Video 3: building in public (18 seconds)

Assets: owned design previews and a deliberately staged, non-sensitive checklist. No fabricated workshop or manufacturing footage.
0-3 seconds: text: `A beautiful render is only the beginning.`
3-9 seconds: checklist: `Sample quality / Real dimensions / Reliable delivery`
9-14 seconds: design preview. Text: `We are checking suppliers before launch.`
14-18 seconds: text: `Follow the process. Help choose the first collection.`
Voiceover: `A beautiful render is only the beginning. Before launch, we need samples, accurate dimensions and reliable delivery. We are checking suppliers now. Follow the process and help choose the first collection.`
Caption: `Building ASTRA STYLIX, one verified step at a time. Designs shown are concepts, not production samples. Follow for sample checks and collection decisions. #BuildingABrand #JewelryDesign #AstraStylix`
Cover: `FROM CONCEPT TO REALITY`

## Seven-day manual pilot

Day 1: Video 1 on both platforms, adapted natively.
Day 2: Instagram Story vote with the same two concepts; respond to relevant comments.
Day 3: Video 2.
Day 4: A short honest answer to a real audience question, if one exists; otherwise show a design detail.
Day 5: Video 3.
Day 6: Show the actual vote result only if enough responses exist; do not invent engagement.
Day 7: compare results and choose the next hook to test.

## X launch sequence

Use X for concise build-in-public updates and qualified replies, not as a duplicate video feed. Keep every claim consistent with the current concept-validation stage.

### Post 1: founding decision

`We are building the first ASTRA STYLIX piece with the people who may actually wear it. Gold or silver? Initial, birthstone, or celestial charm? Vote on Founding Drop 01. No payment, no fake stock - just a real decision before we sample.`

Link: the verified public `/founding-drop` URL.

### Post 2: honest build-in-public update

`A render is not a finished product. Before launch, we are checking compatible components, material specifications, sample quality, assembly time, and real shipping cost. We will share what passes and what does not.`

### Post 3: customer research prompt

`If one piece of jewelry could mark your next chapter, what would it represent: a new beginning, a person, a place, or a private promise?`

Reply only to relevant product questions, purchase-intent comments and collaboration messages. Do not mass-reply to unrelated notifications or use unsolicited DMs.

Record at 24 and 72 hours: views, average watch time, completion where available, saves, shares, substantive comments, profile visits, follows and attributed site visits. Compare rates alongside counts; small samples are directional only. No paid engagement or mass unsolicited messaging.

Website CTA: only after the public site and a working opt-in form have been checked. Instagram currently displays stylixai.com in the supplied screenshot, but its live state has not been verified. Until then use follow/vote CTAs. Do not collect payment for these unverified concepts.
